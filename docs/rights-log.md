All five come from the Prelinger Archives on the Internet Archive. Each item's metadata carries `licenseurl: http://creativecommons.org/licenses/publicdomain/`. That is the archive's statement about the film; it does not clear everything inside it, such as a likeness or a mark. Prelinger's own declaration says only part of the wider collection is public domain, so each item was checked on its own page.

## Known exception

These films show real people and may show real shop signs or products. The owner approved using them on 2026-11-07, as an exception to the "no real people or products" rule, which still applies to everything made for the project. **Before the public release or demo video, a person should watch each clip in full** and replace any that shows a recognisable product or a person in a way that could read as a target of the horror. The events never name or single out anyone in a film. Scares cut away from the film rather than altering it.

## Voices

Presenters speak through the viewer's own browser and system voices (Web Speech API). Nothing is shipped, and nothing is recorded. How a browser generates its voices depends on that browser, so the demo video should be checked against the voice used to record it.
