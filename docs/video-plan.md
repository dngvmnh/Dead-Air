# Demo video plan

Target: under 3 minutes, one continuous take of the live site, with a short title card and
a few voice-over lines added in editing. The video should feel like sitting in front of the
set, not like a feature tour. Let the scares land without commentary over them.

## Before recording

- [ ] Watch each film clip in full (see `docs/rights-log.md`, "Known exception"). Replace any
      clip that shows a recognisable product, or a person who could read as the target of the
      horror.
- [ ] Use a fresh browser profile, or press Forget me twice, so the run starts from Act 0.
- [ ] Desktop Chrome or Edge, 1920x1080, browser zoom 100%. Close other tabs.
- [ ] Record system audio. Volume on the set at full, OS volume at a level that does not
      clip during a jump (do a 10-second test with one startle).
- [ ] Check the presenters' voice. It comes from the browser's own speech voices, so it
      sounds different on each machine. Pick the machine whose voice sounds best.
- [ ] Optional: a webcam for the "Let it see you" moment. Only if the person on camera is
      happy to appear in the video.
- [ ] Recorder: OBS (Display Capture, 60 fps) or the Xbox Game Bar (Win+Alt+R).

## The run

Open `https://dngvmnh.github.io/Dead-Air/?demo=1`. Demo mode compresses the story so the
sign-off arrives in about three minutes. Startles come on their own timer and also answer
clicks, channel changes and tears, so the exact moments vary between takes. Record two or
three takes and keep the best.

| Time | On screen | What you do | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Title card: **DEAD AIR**, "The haunted television network that never goes off the air." | (added in editing) | Sets the tone. |
| 0:08–0:15 | The first-run notice in a dark room | Read it for a beat, click **I understand**. | Shows the jump-scare warning: care for viewers is part of the design. |
| 0:15–0:30 | The set, off. Wallpaper, the note "PLEASE DO NOT PICK AT THE WALLPAPER", a clock showing the real time | Press **POWER**. Warm-up, static, channel 02 news. | Analog TV resurrected: warm-up, tuning, a channel already mid-broadcast. |
| 0:30–0:55 | Channels 02, 04, 07 | **CH+** every few seconds. Pause on the cartoon and the shopping channel. | Always-on linear TV. Changing channel twice is likely to bring the first startle onto the new picture. |
| 0:55–1:20 | The wall beside the set | Drag across the wallpaper. It tears, something is behind it. Let go. | The room is physical. The first tear of a session always answers. |
| 1:20–1:40 | The picture | Drag across the tube. The picture rips; behind it is the inside of the set. | Even the programme can be torn. |
| 1:40–2:05 | Channel 13, then back | Visit 13 (static, test cards). Return to 02. Watch the anchor. | The network starts reading back what you did: visits, order, time. |
| 2:05–2:30 | The room | Keep watching. The lights sink; the wall heaves from behind and rips open by itself. | The rip-back: the room tears at you. Do not narrate over it. |
| 2:30–2:50 | Set off | Press **POWER** off. If a webcam is used: switch **Let it see you** on now, and let the dark glass hold for a few seconds. | The optional reflection, the most personal moment. Skip if no camera. |
| 2:50–3:00 | Sign-off | Power on, go to 13. The station signs off. | The ending, and the hook: it comes back "after hours". |

If the rip-back (2:05) has already happened earlier in the take, keep going: the order of
scenes matters less than the pacing. If the sign-off has not come by 2:50, cut there and
end on the title card.

## Voice-over (optional, over quiet moments only)

1. "This is DEAD AIR. Four channels, always on air, like television used to be."
2. "There's no menu and no objective. You just watch."
3. "But the room is not just a room."
4. "It remembers what you watched. And what you tore."
5. "Everything you see is drawn in code or public domain. Nothing you do leaves your browser."

## Editing checklist

- [ ] Title card at the start, URL card at the end: `dngvmnh.github.io/Dead-Air`.
- [ ] Keep the jumps at full volume: no ducking or music over them.
- [ ] No flashes added in editing. The site keeps to the three-flashes-a-second limit; the
      edit must too.
- [ ] Captions for the voice-over.
- [ ] Final length under 3:00. Export 1080p, H.264.
